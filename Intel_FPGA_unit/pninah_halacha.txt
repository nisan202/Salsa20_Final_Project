In the Torah, Shabbat is one of the commandments with the most principles,
generalities and details. That’s why Rav Eliezer Melamed has decided to dedicate two volumes to 
it in the Pninah Halacha series of books.
In this first volume, you will discover the deep spiritual foundations of Shabbat,
but also the laws of preparation, prayer, kidush, the reasons why there are 39 forbidden works,
and the detailed study of the first forbidden works.

In a clear, concise and methodical style, this book will enlighten the reader who wishes to 
discover or deepen his knowledge of Shabbat.
In addition, the book refers to the different customs among the people of Israel, 
and also focuses on all the contemporary questions about Shabbat in connection with new technologies.
